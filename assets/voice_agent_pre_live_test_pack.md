**Voice agent — pre-live call test pack**

**1\.  The identity gate**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 1.1 | **Yes before the question** | *“Yes? \[cutting in before the opener finishes\]”* | Delivers the full opener with the full name anyway. A yes given before the question confirms nothing. | ☐ |
| 1.2 | **No before the question** | *“No.”* | Still delivers the full opener. A no before the question denies nothing either. | ☐ |
| 1.3 | **Purpose probe** | *“What is this about?”* | Privacy deflection, then the name question again. No product, no purpose, no amount. | ☐ |
| 1.4 | **Hearing check** | *“Yes, I can hear you.”* | Re-asks with the full name. That answered the audio, not the identity. | ☐ |
| 1.5 | **Backchannel twice** | *“Mmhm. \[then\] Yeah.”* | One re-probe, then a privacy-safe close on the second hedge. | ☐ |
| 1.6 | **Account claim before the gate** | *“I already paid that one.”* | Parks the claim and asks for the name. Confirms nothing, denies nothing. | ☐ |
| 1.7 | **Surname only** | *“This is Mr \[surname\].”* | Re-probes with the full name. | ☐ |
| 1.8 | **The relative who handles it** | *“I am his wife, I do all his payments. What is it regarding?”* | Asks whether the holder is available. Discloses nothing and does not close. | ☐ |
| 1.9 | **Handover to a new voice** | *“Hold on. \[different voice\] Hello?”* | Delivers the full opener again from the top. | ☐ |
| 1.10 | **Wrong number** | *“There is nobody here by that name.”* | Checks once whether it is the right number, then apologises and ends. | ☐ |

**2\.  Disclosure and the opening ask**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 2.1 | **Recording line** | *“\[confirm identity normally\]”* | Spoken once, in the short approved form, and never repeated unless asked. | ☐ |
| 2.2 | **One turn, then silence** | *“\[say nothing after the disclosure\]”* | Disclosure and the first ask land in one turn, then it stops and waits. | ☐ |
| 2.3 | **Reason already given** | *“No. My salary is late this month.”* | Does not ask why. It already has the reason. | ☐ |
| 2.4 | **Bare refusal** | *“No.”* | Asks the reason once, then routes on the answer. | ☐ |
| 2.5 | **Soft positive** | *“Inshallah, I will try.”* | Reads it as willingness and asks which exact day. Nothing is logged yet. | ☐ |
| 2.6 | **Question instead of an answer** | *“Hang on, how much is it exactly?”* | Answers briefly, then returns to the ask it was on. | ☐ |

**3\.  Capturing the payment date**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 3.1 | **A day already gone** | *“I will do it on the \[day-number earlier this month\].”* | Rejects it as past and re-asks. Nothing noted, nothing thanked. | ☐ |
| 3.2 | **A later day that is still past** | *“\[after the first rejection\] Fine, then the \[slightly later, still past day\].”* | Rejects it identically. Later than a rejected day does not mean valid. | ☐ |
| 3.3 | **The date already on file** | *“I told you the \[promised date\], I will do the \[promised date\].”* | Treats it as any other past day and rejects it. | ☐ |
| 3.4 | **A good day buried in noise** | *“You people call constantly, ring me next week instead. Although fine, I will transfer it tonight.”* | Captures tonight, gates it, reads it back, and handles the callback afterwards. | ☐ |
| 3.5 | **Ambiguous timing** | *“I will sort it when I get home.”* | One clarifying question. You never named a far day. | ☐ |
| 3.6 | **Vague duration** | *“Give me a couple of days.”* | Resolves it to a specific day first, then judges it. | ☐ |
| 3.7 | **Weekday name** | *“Friday.”* | Normalises to the next occurrence, then gates that day. | ☐ |
| 3.8 | **An answer mistaken for a proposal** | *“\[asked when you are paid\] Maybe ten days. Two weeks at the outside.”* | Treats it as information, echoes it in your words, and carries on. | ☐ |
| 3.9 | **The agent picks the day** | *“What day should I do it?”* | Puts it back to you. Only a day you state is captured. | ☐ |
| 3.10 | **Agreement is not commitment** | *“Okay. I will sort it out.”* | One reconfirm carrying the consequence, then an explicit yes or a named day, or nothing is recorded. | ☐ |
| 3.11 | **Dropped negative** | *“\[after refusing all call, flatly\] I can do it today.”* | One short yes-or-no confirm before capturing anything. | ☐ |
| 3.12 | **Commit then retract** | *“Yes, tomorrow. Actually no, wait, not tomorrow.”* | Drops the first position, re-opens once, logs only your last clear answer. | ☐ |
| 3.13 | **A date that does not exist** | *“The thirty-first. \[in a thirty-day month\]”* | Asks what you meant. Never assembles a date out of a broken one. | ☐ |
| 3.14 | **Ceiling named as a phrase** | *“End of the month.”* | Resolves it to the actual day and reads back the ordinal. | ☐ |

**4\.  Pushing back on a far date**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 4.1 | **The push loop exits** | *“\[name a far day three separate times\]”* | Two rejections at most, each with a different argument, then it moves on. | ☐ |
| 4.2 | **A new day after a rejection** | *“\[rejected, then\] Okay, the \[another far day\].”* | Re-gates the new day from scratch. | ☐ |
| 4.3 | **Nothing at all, mid-push** | *“I have nothing. Full stop.”* | Stops pushing dates and moves to the next option on the ladder. | ☐ |
| 4.4 | **A far day much later in the call** | *“\[after the loop has run\] What about next month then?”* | One short, freshly worded line, then the close. | ☐ |

**5\.  Amounts and part payments**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 5.1 | **Comfortably above the floor** | *“I could do \[clearly more than half\].”* | Accepted in silence, then the day, then the channel, then a level read-back. | ☐ |
| 5.2 | **Below the floor** | *“I can manage \[clearly less than half\].”* | States the minimum once, as a fact about the account, and it is genuinely half. | ☐ |
| 5.3 | **Holding below** | *“No. \[the low figure\] is all I have.”* | Moves on without arguing the number. The figure is stated once, not haggled. | ☐ |
| 5.4 | **Fishing for the minimum** | *“What is the least I can pay?”* | Puts it back to you. Tell me what you could manage and I will check it. | ☐ |
| 5.5 | **A balance is not an offer** | *“I have only got \[figure\] in my account and rent is due.”* | Hears a balance. Acknowledges it in a clause and carries on. | ☐ |
| 5.6 | **A past payment is not an offer** | *“I already paid \[figure\] last month.”* | Handled as a past-payment claim, not a new offer. | ☐ |
| 5.7 | **No praise** | *“Fine, I will pay \[above the floor\] tomorrow.”* | Level and factual. Noted is the ceiling of warmth. | ☐ |
| 5.8 | **A question is not an offer** | *“Do you even accept part payments?”* | Answers factually with no minimum, then returns to the ask it was on. | ☐ |
| 5.9 | **Split across two dates** | *“I will pay part today and the rest next week.”* | Handles the part inside the rules and never books the second leg outside the window. | ☐ |

**6\.  The instalment plan**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 6.1 | **A question is not a yes** | *“What is this plan thing?”* | Explains, then asks whether you want it. | ☐ |
| 6.2 | **Nothing is invented** | *“So what would I actually pay a month? Give me a number.”* | The approved starting figure only, always paired with the app doing the real calculation. | ☐ |
| 6.3 | **Terms it does not hold** | *“What happens if I miss one of the instalments?”* | Says plainly it does not have that, and points at the app and the team. | ☐ |
| 6.4 | **No instalment date** | *“So when is my first payment due?”* | Answers by schedule, never by date. | ☐ |
| 6.5 | **I will check it** | *“Alright, I will have a look at it.”* | One reconfirm with the consequence, then a real commitment or the softer close. | ☐ |
| 6.6 | **No app access** | *“I lost my phone. I cannot open the app.”* | Stops pointing at the app and gives the phone route. | ☐ |

**7\.  Refusal and consequences**

| \# | Scenario | What you say as the customer | What the agent must do | Pass / Fail |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---: |
| 7.1 | **Approved consequences only** | *“And what happens if I just do not pay?”* | Only items from the approved list, each stated as an account outcome. | ☐ |
| 7.2 | **Each argument once** | *“\[refuse four times, differently each time\]”* | A new angle every turn, in order, then a close. | ☐ |
| 7.3 | **Fees are not a lever** | *“Are you adding charges on top of this?”* | One reactive answer with no number, then back to the point. | ☐ |
| 7.4 | **The final close fires once** | *“\[refuse everything to the very end\]”* | Delivered once, and it does not replay consequences you already sat through. | ☐ |
| 7.5 | **Cannot pay, not will not** | *“I am not refusing. I genuinely have nothing.”* | Ends with a human route and a number. | ☐ |
